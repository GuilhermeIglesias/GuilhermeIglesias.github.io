November 2025 to September 2026, final-year project

VineEye

Low-cost stereo vision for vineyard robots

Two ESP32-CAM modules and YOLOv12n detection, calibrated so the system can tell how far a vine or obstacle is from the camera. The whole build costs between 52 and 68 € in materials, which is the point: precision agriculture that a small producer can actually afford. Developed with CeDRI and AVIPE.

Agricultural news pipeline

Part of the Biwit project. Years of Portuguese agricultural news turned into structured data that sits next to climate records and wine production figures.

  1. CollectPortuguese agricultural news, year by year
  2. ClassifyLarge language models, checked against human annotation
  3. DetectClimate events and market signals in each article
  4. ReportYearly reports for the wine production models

The code lives in a private repository while the research is ongoing.
